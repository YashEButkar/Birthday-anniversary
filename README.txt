PHOTO SETUP
1. Open assets/photos/
2. Replace 01.jpg through 12.jpg with your own photos.
3. KEEP THE EXACT FILENAMES: 01.jpg, 02.jpg ... 12.jpg
4. Open photo-mapping.html if you want to move any photo to another memory.
5. Click SAVE PHOTO MAPPING, then open memories.html.

The story contains 12 separate memory chapters. Ekvira Temple and Maple are intentionally separate chapters, while the 12 photos are distributed across the chapters.


IMPORTANT: After replacing 01.jpg-12.jpg, open photo-mapping.html once and assign each photo to its correct memory. Click SAVE PHOTO MAPPING. The Memory Universe will then use that mapping automatically.
