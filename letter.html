06 OCTOBER 2026 · 22ND BIRTHDAY · 2 YEARS OF US

My Akanksha,

Happy 22nd birthday to the person who somehow became one of the most important parts of my life without either of us knowing, on that first ordinary day when I sent you a birthday message on 6 October 2024. When I look back at that day now, it is honestly crazy to think that one small message became the first page of everything we have today. I did not know where that first move would take us. I only knew that I wanted to start a conversation with you. And today, two years later, I am sitting here trying to put two years of memories, feelings, laughter, adventures, arguments, comfort, and love into words — and I already know that no letter can ever be enough.

I still remember our first date at Seawoods Mall on 23 October. There are so many things about that day that I could write about, but the one that always makes me smile is that I made you dance in the mall. Looking back, it feels like such a perfect preview of what our relationship would become: me being a little crazy, you going along with it, and both of us creating a memory that neither of us expected. From there we went to the Chinese Temple, and somehow a simple first date became the beginning of a very long list of places, roads, temples, sunsets, festivals, food stops, and random little moments that now mean so much to me.

We have gone on so many long drives that sometimes I think the road itself became one of our favourite places. I love those moments because we never needed an extraordinary destination to have a good time. Sometimes it was just us, a road ahead, music playing, conversations going nowhere and everywhere, and that comfortable feeling of being together. Those drives taught me that a memory does not have to be a huge event. Sometimes the best memories are the ones where absolutely nothing special is happening — except that you are there.

Then there was the sunset at Marine Drive. There is something about sitting there with someone you love while the sky changes colour and the city keeps moving around you. It makes you stop for a moment. And when I think about that evening, I don't just remember the sunset. I remember how it felt to share it with you. I think that is what you have done to so many ordinary places in my life: you made them personal.

I also can't forget our Ekvira Temple trip in Lonavala and the way that day kept giving us little surprises. On the way back, we stopped at Maple and somehow ended up enjoying free chocolates and snacks like we had discovered treasure. It sounds like such a small thing, but those are exactly the moments I want to keep. The silly ones. The unexpected ones. The ones that would sound completely ordinary if I told someone else, but become special because they happened with you.

Then came Byculla Zoo, another chapter in our growing collection of places. Ambernath Shiv Temple gave us one of my favourite unusual memories — drawing each other's faces on tiny pieces of paper. I don't know how two people can turn a small sheet of paper into something worth remembering years later, but apparently we can. Kala Ghoda Festival in CST became another story, another day of walking around, seeing things, laughing, exploring, and simply being together.

And then I got to be part of your sister's Haldi ceremony in your village. That day was different. It was not just another outing for us. I got to see another part of your world, meet people important to you, and be there for a family celebration. We enjoyed so much, and I remember feeling genuinely happy that I could be included in something that mattered to you and your family. Moments like that make me realise that our story isn't only about dates and trips. It is also about slowly becoming part of each other's worlds.

Gudi Padwa is another memory I hold close. Seeing us both dressed traditionally, visiting temples, and spending that day together felt beautiful in a completely different way. There was something peaceful about it. Looking at those memories now, I realise how much has changed since that first birthday message — and at the same time, how much of the same feeling is still there.

Two years with you have taught me that love is not only about the dramatic moments. It is about the small things that quietly become important. It is remembering a joke that only makes sense to us. It is knowing how the other person reacts to something before they even say it. It is sending a random message. It is laughing at something stupid. It is going for a drive without a perfect plan. It is getting irritated, getting over it, and still choosing each other. It is making plans, changing plans, and sometimes making absolutely no plans at all. It is finding comfort in the presence of one person.

Akanksha, I want you to know that I am proud of the person you are becoming. You are turning 22, and I hope this year gives you more reasons to smile, more opportunities to grow, more adventures than you expect, and the courage to chase everything you want. I hope you never feel like you have to become someone else to be worthy of anything. I hope you always remember how much there is to love about the person you already are.

I also want to thank you. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for the patience. Thank you for the laughter. Thank you for the random conversations and the serious ones. Thank you for every long drive, every temple visit, every sunset, every festival, every food stop, every photo, every silly drawing, every celebration, and every ordinary day that became special simply because you were there. Thank you for being part of these two years of my life.

When we started on 6 October 2024, I couldn't have imagined that two years later I would have this many memories to look back on. And the best part is that I don't want this letter to feel like a conclusion. I don't want these two years to be a finished story. I want them to be the first two chapters of something much, much longer.

I want more sunsets with you. More long drives. More random plans. More festivals. More temples. More food adventures. More photos that we will laugh about years later. More days where we have no idea where we are going and somehow end up somewhere memorable. More birthdays. More celebrations. More ordinary evenings that turn into favourite memories. More versions of us that we haven't met yet.

And if there is one thing I hope you take from this letter, it is this: you matter to me. Not because of one perfect moment, not because of one date, and not because of one photograph. You matter because somewhere between that first message and everything that followed, you became part of the way I see my life. When something funny happens, I want to tell you. When I see something beautiful, I want to share it with you. When I think about places I want to go, I imagine you there. And when I think about the memories I want to make in the future, your name somehow keeps appearing in them.

So today, on your 22nd birthday, I hope you feel loved. I hope you feel celebrated. I hope you look at this little world I made for you and realise that behind every animation, every photo, every word, and every tiny detail is one very simple thought: I am really glad I made that first move.

Happy 22nd birthday, Akanksha. Happy two years to us. Thank you for being one of the most beautiful chapters of my life so far. And here's to every chapter we haven't written yet.

Always yours,
Yash ♡
